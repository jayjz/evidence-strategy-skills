# Source policy

Status: P1 V0 contract, 2026-09-13. Apply sources to claims; source prestige is
not a substitute for relevance, access, or critical reading.

## Claim-specific source choices

| Claim being made | Preferred evidence | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What a strategy framework says | Original publication, then authoritative university/research-institution explanation; scholarly critiques or replications for limitations | Theory supplies constructs and proposed mechanisms, not facts about today's target company. Distinguish conceptual argument, case illustration, and empirical test. |
| What a company offers or documents | Current official documentation, specifications, release notes, dated pricing and filings | Attribute marketing and self-reported performance. A vendor comparison is not independent comparative evidence. Record plan, geography, date, and version when relevant. |
| How a product/runtime behaves | Reproducible observation plus current official documentation | A single observed run cannot establish reliability; a bug report is a report until reproduced. Preserve documentation/observation disagreement. |
| Market size, adoption, or commercial behavior | Public records, original datasets, disclosed surveys, transaction/usage evidence with appropriate access | Inspect population, units, geography, period, sampling, missingness, definitions, sponsor, and calculation. Do not mix users, seats, downloads, revenue, and firms. |
| What customers/community members report | Direct post, issue, review, interview, or contextual observation | Match the claim to the speaker and episode. Self-selection, promotion, duplicates, moderation, and unknown identities limit generalization. |
| Analytical conclusion or recommendation | Explicit premises linked to evidence, alternatives, assumptions, and a falsifiable next check | Label inference. A model's recollection, another generated report, or a framework does not supply independent evidence. |

## Strategy theory in V0

Five Forces concerns industry structure and value distribution; use the
industry as the unit of analysis before applying its categories. HBS's Institute
for Strategy and Competitiveness traces it to Porter's 1979 article and explains
its forces. This is an authoritative conceptual reference, not evidence that a
particular entrant will succeed.
[HBS, The Five Forces](https://www.isc.hbs.edu/strategy/business-strategy/Pages/the-five-forces.aspx).

For customer jobs, HBS Online's interview assignment focuses on a purchase,
its initiating circumstance, and alternatives considered. That supports a
procedure for gathering situated accounts. V0's inference is that a social
post lacking that history leaves the proposed job provisional; an interview
account alone also does not identify a causal effect.
[HBS Online, Jobs to be Done interview assignment](https://info.email.online.hbs.edu/disruptive-strategy-jobs-to-be-done-interview-instructions).

When a later task needs resource-based strategy, dynamic capabilities, or
another lens, find the original formulation and relevant critiques at that
time. Do not populate every framework directory during P1. Record authors,
year, title, venue, DOI or stable URL, inspected sections, assumptions, and the
specific use of the construct. Distinguish later practitioner adaptations from
the original theory. Highly cited does not mean universally applicable or
empirically established.

## Acquisition and citation rules

1. State the decision, claim, scope, and evidence needed before choosing the
   framework or searching. Set a bounded collection and stopping rule.
2. Discover broadly enough to encounter contrary accounts. Prefer original
   records; follow citations upstream. If only a secondary source is accessible,
   cite that source as secondary and state the access limitation.
3. Read the relevant body, section, table, or passage. A title, search snippet,
   AI summary, or bibliography hit cannot support details that were not read.
   If only an abstract is accessible, restrict the claim to it.
4. Retain a direct link and precise locator with publication/update date when
   available, retrieval date, context, brief passage/paraphrase, and limitations
   using the [evidence contract](evidence-standard.md). Resolve redirects and
   distinguish document revision from the date of the event described.
5. Put each citation beside the claim it supports. Separate multiple assertions
   when one source supports only some of them. Cite an original dataset and show
   calculations for a derived number, including units and assumptions.
6. Record disagreement, inaccessible sources, source dependence, and exclusions.
   A press release copied by five publications remains one originating account.
   Do not resolve conflict by majority vote over URLs.

Current runtime claims require current official documentation and later a
versioned test for any promise of support. Stable theory can use an older
original source. Date-sensitive company, market, and community claims require
a stated observation window and relevance check; a universal "fresh within
30 days" rule would be inappropriate across these claim types.

## Publication and reuse

Store only the evidence needed to audit a conclusion. Use short attributed
extracts or faithful paraphrases within applicable access and reuse terms.
Do not redistribute full restricted papers, personal data, or private community
content. If a legally retainable snapshot is unavailable, preserve the locator,
minimal extract, retrieval date, and limitation; do not claim full replayability.
The [security standard](security-standard.md) governs retention and redaction.
